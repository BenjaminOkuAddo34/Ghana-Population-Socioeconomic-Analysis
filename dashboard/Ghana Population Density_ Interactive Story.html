Ghana has 30.8 million people, and they are packed very unevenly.

Greater Accra holds about 1,473 people on every square kilometre. Savannah holds about 19. That is a 78× gap inside one country.

This page walks through my analysis of the 2021 census across 16 regions and 261 districts, built with Python and GIS.
